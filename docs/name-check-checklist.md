# Name check checklist: Clayfolk (runner-up: Claycrew)

Goal: knock out obvious conflicts before you spend money on a domain. This is a knockout search, not legal clearance. If anything looks close, note it and decide; if the product ever becomes paid, have a trademark attorney clear the name.

Time: about 60-90 minutes. Do Clayfolk first; only do Claycrew if Clayfolk fails.

## 1. Trademark registers (you run these)

Search each term below: `Clayfolk`, `Clay Folk`, `Clayfolks`, and sound-alikes `Clayfok`, `Claifolk`. Record hits in the table at the bottom.

| Register | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| USPTO | uspto.gov, "Search our trademark database" | Search "live" and "dead". Use "contains" for the term. |
| EUIPO | euipo.europa.eu/eSearch | Covers the EU, which includes Sweden. |
| WIPO Global Brand Database | branddb.wipo.int | International registrations. |
| PRV (Sweden) | prv.se, trademark search | Only needed if EUIPO is unclear. |

Classes that matter:
- 9: downloadable software and digital images
- 42: software as a service, design services
- 41: entertainment, education
- 16: printed matter, art and craft supplies
- 28: toys and games

Rule of thumb: an exact or near-identical mark in 9, 42 or 41 is a stop. In 16 or 28 it is a yellow flag: look at who owns it and what they sell. A dead (abandoned) filing does not clear the name but is not a blocker.

## 3. Domain (only after everything above is clean)

- Registry lookup showed `clayfolk.dev`, `.app`, `.design`, `.io` and `.se` unregistered on 2026-11-11. `.com` is registered.
- Check the price at Cloudflare Registrar before buying. Premium-priced names are sometimes held back.

## 4. Decision rule

- Clean or only yellow flags in toys/crafts: go. Claim npm and the repo, then buy the domain.
- Exact or near-identical mark in classes 9, 42 or 41: stop and switch to the runner-up.
- Unsure: write down the hit and who owns it, and ask before spending money.
